Core Features by Module

Key modules support the full business cycle from sales to finance.

Majjun ERP organizes core functionality into operational modules. The Sales & Accounts Receivable module manages the revenue cycle from customer orders to payment recording.

Sales & Accounts Receivable (AR)

Order-to-Payment Flow

Manages the standard revenue process, including inventory checks, invoicing, and payment.

  • Create customer and order
  • Check stock and reservations
  • Generate invoice and record cash receipt
Sales & Accounts Receivable (AR)

Direct Invoicing

Allows billing users to create an invoice directly for one-off sales or services without a prior sales order.

  • Create direct invoices
  • Validate customer credit terms
  • Post to the General Ledger
Sales & Accounts Receivable (AR)

AI Sales Assistance

The AI assistant integrates directly into the invoicing process.

  • Suggest tax codes for invoice line items
  • Validate non-conformance
  • Support billing decisions during invoice creation
Sales & Accounts Receivable (AR)

Customer Master

Stores all customer records, contact information, and billing terms.

  • Create customer records
  • Read, update and delete customer records
  • Maintain contact information and billing terms
Procurement & Accounts Payable (AP)

Procurement Workflow

Supports the full PR-to-PO-to-GR process for purchasing goods and inventory replenishment.

  • Create Purchase Requisitions (PR)
  • Route approval requests up to auto-approval limits
  • Convert to Purchase Orders and record Goods Receipts
Procurement & Accounts Payable (AP)

AI Expense Assistance

An AI agent can propose common recurring expenses and draft the corresponding AP entries.

  • Identify recurring vendor expenses
  • Draft corresponding AP entries
  • Send suggestions to a review queue for approval and posting
Procurement & Accounts Payable (AP)

Expense & Payment Flows

Manages the creation and tracking of AP invoices and payments to vendors.

  • Create and track AP invoices
  • Support part-payments to suppliers
  • Track outstanding payable balances
Inventory & Items

Item Master

Tracks all items and services offered by the business.

  • Store item type for service or inventory
  • Maintain cost and SKU data
  • Track stock levels by warehouse
Inventory & Items

Stock Management

Tracks current inventory and handles transactions that affect stock.

  • Support a basic warehouse stock model
  • Use stock adjustment APIs
  • Reserve inventory for sales orders
Inventory & Items

Inventory Ledger

Maintains a separate ledger to track inventory valuation and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).

  • Record inventory ledger updates
  • Post COGS during sales workflows
  • Hook valuation updates into procurement flows
Finance Core & Reporting

General Ledger (GL)

The backbone of all financial postings from transactional flows.

  • Manage Chart of Accounts (COA)
  • Map postings from subledgers
  • Post and reverse manual or automated Journal Entries
Finance Core & Reporting

Financial Reports

Provides on-demand and scheduled reports for financial analysis.

  • Trial Balance, Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet
  • COGS reports
  • Export capabilities for finance teams
Finance Core & Reporting

Audit Trail

Comprehensive logging of all transactional and system changes.

  • Log who, what, when and why for transactional changes
  • Track system changes and agent actions
  • Enable quick audit queries

Technical Implementation

Modern standards for scalability, security and development efficiency.

Majjun ERP is built on robust technology choices that support high-performance APIs, multi-tenant data isolation, containerized deployment and AI-powered agents.

Backend Framework

.NET 10 (LTS), ASP.NET Core MVC

Provides a modern, high-performance foundation for web applications and APIs.

Database

PostgreSQL 15+

Optimized for concurrent operations, supporting multi-tenant isolation through row-level security checks.

Deployment

Render / Azure Services

Uses a containerized CI/CD pipeline for automated and consistent deployment.

AI Integration

OpenAI API or Azure OpenAI

Powers Large Language Model integration for both Business Intelligence and Code Generation agents.
